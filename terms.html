1. What Tracka is, and what it isn’t

Tracka helps people find cards at trading-card shows. Vendors publish the cards they plan to bring to a show, collectors track their collections and can share the cards they’re bringing, and anyone can browse, favorite and message.

Tracka is not a marketplace. You can’t buy or sell a card in the app, and there’s no cart, payment, shipping or escrow. Any deal you arrange happens in person, at your own judgment, directly with the other person. We aren’t a party to it and aren’t responsible for it. The only thing you can pay for in the app is Tracka Pro (section 8).

Prices in the app are either prices a person typed in or market values from Scrydex, our catalog and pricing provider. Market values come from sales and listings elsewhere, and they can be wrong or out of date. A card is worth what someone will actually pay for it. Treat every number in the app as information, not as an appraisal or investment advice.

5. Safety tools and moderation

  • Block someone from the menu in your conversation with them. Blocking works both ways: they can’t message you, and you disappear from each other’s collections, favorites and inbox. You can unblock people in Settings, Privacy, Blocked users.
  • Report a message by pressing and holding it. The app offers to block the sender at the same time.
  • Report a profile or a table from the menu at the top of that screen.
  • Report anything else through Settings, About, Report a problem, or email support@gettracka.com.

We review reports promptly, usually within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and, where it’s warranted, the accounts that posted it.

8. Tracka Pro

Tracka is free to download and use. Tracka Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds:

  • Unlimited scanning. Without Pro you get 25 card scans and 10 slab scans in any rolling seven days. A slab counts as a slab scan in any scan mode.
  • Price alerts: a push when a card hits your price. Without Pro you can watch 3 cards at a time.
  • Collection report and export: what your cards gained since you added them, and a spreadsheet of your whole collection.
  • Master set binders: every card of a set in its pocket, and the cost to finish it. Without Pro you get one master set whole, plus the 30th Celebration set, and a preview of the others.
  • Full price history: every range on every card’s price graph. Without Pro, graphs cover up to three months.
  • Real card photos: photos of your own cards in place of the catalog art.
  • Real slab photos: PSA’s own photo on every PSA slab you add. Without Pro, your first 10 PSA slabs get one and the rest show a drawn slab.
  • The Pro badge on your profile, your Base and your tables.

The purchase terms:

  • Price. $29.99 once in the US. Apple sets prices in other regions and shows them at checkout.
  • Billing. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. We never see or handle your card details.
  • Lifetime. Pro stays on your account for as long as Tracka runs. Nothing renews and there’s nothing to cancel. Pro covers the Pro features the app offers at the time, which can change as the app does.
  • Fair use. Scanning is for your own cards. If an account’s scanning looks automated, or goes far past what one person’s collection needs, we may pause it for a while.
  • Refunds. Apple handles purchases, so refund requests go to Apple. A refunded purchase turns Pro off.
  • Restoring. The purchase belongs to the Apple ID that bought it. “Restore purchases” in the app brings it back on another device.

Monthly and yearly subscriptions. Pro used to be sold as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription. If you still have one, it keeps renewing through Apple until you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends, in iOS Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. Your Apple ID is charged for each renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends, and after you turn it off you keep Pro until the paid period runs out.

Pro gifts. Sometimes we give Pro at no charge. A gift never charges you.

If Pro ends for any reason, your account and your whole collection stay put. Only the Pro features switch off.
